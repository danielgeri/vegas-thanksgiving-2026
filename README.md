## ✨ Features
- **100% Offline Support**: Service Worker (`sw.js`) caches all trip data, confirmations, and guides for offline access on the plane or inside Red Rock Canyon.
- **One-Tap Calendar Export (`.ics`)**: Add individual or all confirmed flights and hotel stays to Apple/Google Calendar.
- **Interactive 6-Day Itinerary**: Add custom group plans and notes saved to `localStorage`.
- **Large-Group Restaurant Explorer**: Filter 16+ vetted restaurants by neighborhood (Summerlin/Durango near the resort, Chinatown, The Strip), Thanksgiving availability, and kid-friendliness.
- **Events & Attractions Explorer**: Thanksgiving Week 2026 concerts (The Eagles & Wizard of Oz at Sphere, Journey, Jonas Brothers), Enchant Christmas at Las Vegas Ballpark (10 mins from resort), NCAA Players Era Festival, and family activities.
